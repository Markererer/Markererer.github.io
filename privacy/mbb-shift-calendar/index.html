Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MBB Shift Calendar processes information when you use the Chrome extension.

Information processed

MBB Shift Calendar processes the MBB login credentials that you enter in the extension so it can access your MBB schedule. The email address may be stored locally in your browser if you choose the remember-login option. The password is stored locally only when you explicitly enable that option. Otherwise, the password is discarded after the sync.

The extension processes assigned and available shift information from the MBB schedule, including the details needed to create corresponding calendar events. It also processes the relevant Google Calendar event information needed to check for duplicates.

How information is used

MBB shift information is used only to create events in the separate MBB - Assigned Shifts and MBB - Available Shifts Google Calendars. Existing calendar events are checked so the extension can avoid creating duplicates. The extension displays sync progress and error messages to help you understand the result of a sync.

The extension does not sell user data. It does not use data for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Local browser storage

Any remembered email address and any password saved through the explicit password option are stored locally by the extension in your browser. The extension has no separate backend server. You can remove locally stored information by disabling the relevant option, clearing the extension's stored data, or uninstalling the extension.

Google Calendar data

Google Calendar access is requested through Google OAuth. Calendar data is used only to create MBB shift events and check for duplicate events in the two MBB shift calendars. The extension does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, analytics, tracking, sale, or any unrelated purpose.

Third-party communication

Data is communicated only with the MBB website and the Google Calendar API as needed for the stated functionality. The MBB website is used to retrieve the schedule associated with your account. Google is used to authorize access and create or inspect the relevant calendar events. No separate service operated by the extension receives or stores this data.

User control and revocation

You choose whether to enable the remember-login options and whether to authorize Google Calendar access. You may revoke the extension's Google access from your Google Account security settings. You can also remove the extension's local browser storage or uninstall the extension. Revoking access may prevent future calendar syncs, but it does not delete events already created in your Google Calendars; those can be managed directly in Google Calendar.
